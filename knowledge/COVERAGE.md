# Coverage map

The basic legal questions people living in Switzerland actually have, grouped by area of life and prepared in waves. A situation counts as covered only once it is published after lawyer review; drafts are listed but not counted.

## Rules for every situation

- German master text first. French, Italian, Romansh and English follow after review.
- Every rule, warning, deadline and cost statement cites an official source, verified by hash.
- Deadlines come from the deadline engine, never from prose.
- "Unknown" is always a valid answer. When in doubt, the situation points to personal advice and free help.
- No references to commentaries or other secondary works.
